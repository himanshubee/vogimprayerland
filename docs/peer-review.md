# Peer review

Date: 20 November 2026

## Method

Six public ministry websites were read through their public pages. Only visible text returned by those pages is recorded. “Not seen” means the item was not present in the fetched page text; it does not prove the feature is absent elsewhere. No copy, images or branding were reused. Lighthouse was not available because no Chrome/Chromium executable is installed on the audit machine.

Peers:

1. [Pastor Jerry Eze / Streams of Joy](https://jerryeze.com/)

4. [RCCG](https://www.rccg.org/)

## Lessons applied

- Keep service times, locations and online joining visible and direct, as seen on Elevation, RCCG and Hillsong.
- Keep a named leader and a clear ministry story, as seen on Jerry Eze's site and RCCG.
- Treat live and replay access as core navigation rather than a hidden archive.
- Do not copy peer claims, statistics or testimony treatment. VOGIM's stronger differentiators are free-prayer wording, optional sensitive details, consent-based media, a scam notice and checkable official channels.
